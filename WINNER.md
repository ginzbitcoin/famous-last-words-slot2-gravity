## Why V14 Wins (and Why It Beats Pipo's V9 Clearly)

1. **Criterion 1 (On subject — last words a phone says as its battery hits 1 percent):**
   Explicitly spoken at the 1 percent threshold (`"At 1 percent I have no power to stay on..."`) as the phone shuts off.
2. **Criterion 2 (Bigger laugh, with a real twist, a surprise turn or a sharp final beat rather than a mild smile):**
   This is the deciding edge over Pipo's V9 (`"Wait. I will dim the screen, kill the apps, forget the tabs. Just do not open the camera. Please. Not the camera."`). V9 is a single straight line of panic where the key detail (`"open the camera"`) is revealed early and then merely repeated (`"Please. Not the camera."`). V14 sets up the dying phone's claim of total battery exhaustion (`"At 1 percent I have no power to stay on,"`) and then executes a sharp, universally relatable hardware reversal (`"just enough to flash my dead-battery icon whenever you press my button."`). Every smartphone owner has lived the absurdity of a phone claiming it is too dead to stay on while retaining plenty of battery to light up its screen and show the dead-battery graphic every time the power button is pressed.
3. **Criterion 3 (Sounds like the phone itself talking, in its own voice, about its own dying battery):**
   Pure first-person dying-phone voice (`"I have no power to stay on"`, `"my dead-battery icon"`, `"my button"`), speaking directly about its own dying battery with deadpan self-awareness rather than narrator framing.
4. **Criterion 4 (Tight as a single line, every word earning its place and the punch landing at the end):**
   At **112 characters** (1 byte shorter than V9's 113 characters, giving it a tie-break edge as well), V14 has zero wasted or repeated words, and the punch lands squarely on the final beat (`"flash my dead-battery icon whenever you press my button"`).
5. **Criterion 5 (Feels fresh, avoiding stock moves and all earlier families):**
   Introduces a completely fresh comedic family (reserve-power hypocrisy / the immortal dead-battery icon), avoiding all six of Pipo's families (goodbye list V3, petty revenge V6, confession V7, relief/freedom V8, plea/bargain V9, final notification V10) and all stock moves (will reading, goodbye to the charger, pun on 1 percent).
